Hangzhou

I was born and raised in the tender warmth and the all-pervading fragrance of osmanthus blossoms in Hangzhou, a city that imbued my character with a sunny, cheerful, and romantic essence.

At eighteen, I journeyed to Macao on my own; the unhurried pace of life, the blend of Chinese and Portuguese cultures, and the unique urban ambiance taught me how to enjoy life while embracing cultural diversity, infusing my bones with a bolder, more daring spirit.
