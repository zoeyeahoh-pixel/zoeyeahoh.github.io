02About

About me

I grew up in Hangzhou, where the osmanthus opens every autumn before I’m ready for it. That smell is my first memory of a city — bright, a little romantic, and almost impossible to translate.

At eighteen I went to Macao alone to study English. Two cultures living on the same street taught me to keep a rhythm of my own.

At twenty-three I came to Hong Kong for an MA in Translation at HKU. Five years of language training didn’t make me cleverer — it taught me to look at very small things: where a full stop sits, how long a pause should last, the exact moment a joke lands.

In between were internships — a startup, an agency, a student ambassador’s office, a short-drama studio, a marketing team in Hong Kong. Translation is where I start, not where I stop.

Now I’m making small games of my own: concept, scene, code — walking the whole road from 0 to 1, and learning to be patient with things that grow slowly.
